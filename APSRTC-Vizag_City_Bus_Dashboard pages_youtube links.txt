APSRTC-Vizag_City_Bus_Fleet_Analysis
APSRTC Vizag City Bus Fleet Analysis. The Analysis is done taking APSRTC Yearly RTI into consideration. RTI obtained contain data of APSRTC's total state fleet but not limited to Visakhapatnam. As a result most important parameters are deduced from RTI, Internet & Levelled them down to Visakhapatnam City.

Executive page:
This Page describes the total number of City buses,Active routes,Daily passengers, Earning/KM, Cost/km, km operated/day, Daily revenue, Fleet Utilization%,Daily passenger trend, Fleet Vs Revenue share%

Bus_Fleet Page
The Page describes the total number of buses, maintenance buses,effective fleet operating,km/bus/day ,revenue/day,buses by engine type, total number of buses with depots (active,spare and workshop), bus types (city ordinary/metro express/luxury) based on Engines and buses manufactured yearwise

Depot_Scorecard
The Page describes the Total number of Depots, Bus stops/ shelters, service reliability%, fare box recovery ratio, cost/km,revenue depot wise, Total buses(Owned/Hired) depot wise , Routes operated, Fleet utilization%,Revenue share%, Daily passengers count depot wise

Routes,Revenue & Operations
The Page describes total number of trips, departure punctuality%, breakdown rate, avg trip delay, trips on time, total GPS fitted buses, Top 10 routes bu passenger count,Top 10 routes by revenue generation, Routes coverage by distance band and time taken to cover the routes time band
